We collect personal information in the following ways.

Information from third parties

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide insurance consultations, quotes, applications, and policy service
  • Verify identity, licensing, and eligibility, and to underwrite or place coverage with carriers
  • Communicate with you by phone, text, email, mail, or social media, including appointment reminders
  • Recruit, evaluate, contract, appoint, train, and pay independent agents and producers
  • Operate, secure, and improve our website, advertising, and business operations
  • Measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and understand which ads, pages, or forms led to a contact
  • Comply with insurance, tax, anti-money-laundering, recordkeeping, and other legal obligations
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

We do not use sensitive information, such as health data, to train third-party artificial-intelligence models. We use sensitive information only as needed to provide insurance services, comply with law, or as you otherwise direct.

We and our partners may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, understand traffic, and deliver or measure ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may limit some site or ad features. You can also use platform controls such as Facebook Ad Preferences, Off-Facebook Activity, and industry opt-outs such as aboutads.info and networkadvertising.org.

Federal law, including the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, and state insurance privacy laws restrict how insurance agencies may use and disclose nonpublic personal information about customers and consumers. This section is our consumer privacy notice for insurance-related information.

We collect nonpublic personal information from you, from your transactions with us, and from insurance companies, consumer-reporting agencies, and other third parties, as described in Section 2. We may disclose that information to insurance companies and other third parties as needed to provide the products and services you request, to service policies, and as otherwise permitted or required by law (for example, processing, servicing, underwriting, claims, audits, licensing, and compliance).

We do not disclose nonpublic personal information about our customers or former customers to nonaffiliated third parties except as described in this Privacy Policy or as otherwise permitted by law. If state law gives you additional rights regarding insurance information, we will honor those rights. Health and medical information obtained for underwriting is used and disclosed only as needed to place or service coverage, as required by law, or with appropriate authorization.
